Founder and builder of Great Lakes Levels · Bay City, Michigan

Chris Izworski built Great Lakes Levels as a free public-data project for shoreline communities, property owners, paddlers, boaters, and anyone trying to understand current Great Lakes water levels in historical context. The site combines official lake-level data and long-term records with pages organized around the questions people actually ask about the water near them.

What this project is for: make official Great Lakes level data easier to read without hiding the source, the historical context, or the limits of what the data can tell you.

Why Great Lakes Levels exists

Chris lives in the Bay City, Michigan area on Saginaw Bay, where water-level swings are not abstract. They change beaches, marsh edges, docks, shoreline exposure, navigation, and the way property owners think about the next storm season. Great Lakes Levels grew from wanting a clearer way to compare the present lake level with previous high- and low-water periods and to see that information at a useful local scale.

The project relies on public agency sources rather than private estimates. Its job is to organize those records into a readable decision layer while keeping the original data sources visible. It is not a substitute for engineering, legal, regulatory, insurance, or property advice.

About Chris Izworski

Chris Izworski's professional background is in public-safety operations, emergency communications, and public-safety technology. He spent more than a decade leading county 911 operations in Michigan before moving into technology work supporting emergency communications. That experience shaped the design philosophy behind Great Lakes Levels: reliable inputs, visible provenance, clear limitations, and a useful answer for a person who does not want to reverse-engineer the underlying data feeds.

His broader work spans Great Lakes information projects, Michigan outdoor data tools, gardening and seed saving, writing, and public-safety technology. The complete source-backed biography and professional record are maintained on Chris Izworski's verified profile.
